# User Guide

## 1. Signing in

1. Open the helpdesk address you were given and sign in with your
   username and password.
2. **First sign-in:** your administrator gave you a temporary password.
   You will be taken straight to **Change password**: enter the temporary
   password, then your own new password twice. Nothing else is available
   until you do.

Password rules: at least 16 characters, not containing your username,
and not a common or breached password. Long pass phrases with spaces are
welcome, and password managers work.

* **Forgot your password?** Ask your administrator to reset it. There is
  no email reset.
* **Locked out after several tries?** Five attempts per minute are
  allowed. Wait one minute.
* You are signed out automatically **8 hours** after signing in.
* **Logout** signs you out on all your devices.
* **Password** (top menu) changes your password and signs you out on your other devices.

## 2. For everyone: raising and following tickets

### Raise a ticket

**New Ticket** → title, description, priority → **Submit Ticket**.

| Priority | Use for | First-response target |
|---|---|---|
| Critical | Many people cannot work | 1 hour |
| High | You cannot work | 4 hours |
| Medium | Work is harder but possible | 24 hours |
| Low | Questions and small requests | 72 hours |

The targets are the helpdesk's current example policy (calendar hours).
Your organisation may change them.

Do not put passwords or other secrets in a ticket.

### Follow your tickets

The **dashboard** lists your tickets with their status and SLA state. Use
**search** (title and description) and the **status**, **priority** and
**SLA** filters; pages hold 10 tickets each. Open a ticket to see its
comments and its **activity history** (created, assigned, status changes,
comments), newest first.

Add information in the comment box on the ticket page and press **Post Note**.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Open | Waiting for the helpdesk |
| In Progress | A technician is working on it |
| Resolved | The technician believes it is fixed; comment if it is not |
| Closed | Finished |

Only technicians and administrators change status. You can comment on
any of your tickets.

### Export

**Export CSV** downloads the tickets you can see, with the current search
and filters applied.

### Email notifications

When email is enabled you are told when your ticket is created, receives a
staff reply, or is resolved or closed. Emails contain the ticket number
and status, never the description or comments; sign in to read those.
**During the pilot, email may be switched off**; your administrator will
tell you.

## 3. For technicians and administrators

You see **all** tickets.

* **Dashboard SLA cards** count tickets by SLA state. Click a card to
  filter:

  | SLA state | Meaning |
  |---|---|
  | Within SLA | No staff response yet; deadline not near |
  | Due Soon | Deadline within 30 minutes |
  | Overdue | Deadline passed with no staff response |
  | Met | First staff comment was before the deadline |
  | Breached | First staff comment was after the deadline |
  | Not Tracked | The ticket has no SLA record |

* **The first comment by a technician or administrator** counts as the
  response. Changing the status does not.
* **Update a ticket:** on the ticket page choose the **Status** and
  **Assign To**, then **Save Changes**. Only active technicians and
  administrators can be assigned.
* An assignee shown as "no longer eligible" left or changed role; choose a
  new one.
